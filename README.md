## 🎯 What it is

**Aria** is a live show generator. You open a room, give it a topic, and an episode is written from nothing: the cast, their personalities, and the whole script. Each line is then **spoken aloud** and streamed to your browser, where two 3D robot hosts perform it with subtitles and a live level meter.

Nobody wrote the jokes in advance. You typed a subject and pressed go.

## ✨ What you get

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🎬 A whole episode, on any topic
Type *"why pineapple belongs on pizza"* or *"the history of the paperclip"* and get a full show — cast invented to fit the subject, roughly two dozen lines, start to finish.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🗣️ They actually speak
Every line is voiced. Distinct voices per host, male and female, with subtitles colour-coded to whoever's talking and a meter that moves with the audio.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🤖 Design your own host
A wizard for the name, the personality and the voice — ending in a 3D twin of the robot you just built, rendered live.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🎓 English practice mode
A completely different show: a **coach**, a **learner** and a **guide**. The learner slips up, the coach corrects and explains, the guide offers a more natural phrasing, the learner tries again. Pick your scenario and level.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🎨 Fifteen themes
A swatch picker with real colour chips, plus ambient atmosphere — drifting petals, floating embers, slow stars — that quietens itself if you've asked your device to reduce motion.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 📻 A radio station
A full station of tracks that plays on its own, with no accounts, no keys and no setup at all.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🔁 The show doesn't stop
If one part of the machinery is unavailable or rate-limited, the next one picks it up mid-episode. You hear a podcast, not an error message.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🎚️ Choose the writer
A settings panel lets you pick which intelligence writes the next show. Change it and the very next episode is written differently — no restart.

</td>
</tr>
</table>

## 🎧 How an episode happens

```
        you type a topic
                │
                ▼
        ✍️  the script is written  ── cast · personalities · ~24 lines
                │
                ▼
        🗣️  every line is voiced  ── a distinct voice per host
                │
                ▼
        📡  streamed to your browser as it's ready
                │
                ▼
        🤖  robots perform it  ── subtitles · level meter · your theme
```

<div align="center"><sub>From a blank field to two robots arguing: about a minute.</sub></div>
